Frequently Asked Questions

Which smart home devices does HomeLock work with?

HomeLock works with any HomeKit-compatible outlet, switch, or light. This includes brands like Eve, Meross, Wemo, TP-Link Kasa (HomeKit models), Aqara, and many others. Cameras, doorbells, sensors, smart locks, garage doors, blinds, AC units, faucets, and sprinklers are not supported because the on/off lock paradigm doesn't apply to them. Lutron Caséta devices aren't supported in this version.

Why don't I see all of my HomeKit devices?

By design, HomeLock filters out non-supported categories (cameras, sensors, smart locks, etc.). If you expect a device to appear and it's missing, make sure it's added to your Home in Apple's Home app first, and that it identifies as an outlet, switch, or light category.

The lock command failed with an error.

Most failures come from the smart-home accessory itself. If you consistently see errors with a specific device:

  • Open the Apple Home app and confirm the device is reachable there.
  • Reset the accessory by unplugging it for 30 seconds.
  • If the issue persists, email us with the device brand, model, and a screenshot of the error.

I forgot my PIN.

For security, HomeLock cannot recover a forgotten PIN. To reset, uninstall and reinstall the app. Your locks and schedules will resync from iCloud once you sign back in. Note that uninstalling does NOT remove your iCloud data.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

HomeLock Pro is a one-time $9.99 purchase, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. To request a refund, follow Apple's standard refund process at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does HomeLock work without internet?

Locking and unlocking devices on your local network works offline as long as your iPhone can reach the smart-home accessories directly. iCloud sync and Siri Shortcuts that route through Apple require an active internet connection.

Do schedules work when the app is closed?

A schedule starts a lock only while HomeLock is running, either in the foreground or in the background. Once a lock has started, it always ends on time, even if the app is closed.

How do I delete all my HomeLock data?

Uninstall the app to remove on-device data. To also remove iCloud data, go to Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → HomeLock and tap Delete Data.

System Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 26.2 or later
  • A HomeKit home with at least one compatible outlet, switch, or light
  • iCloud account (only for syncing your own history and schedules across your devices)
